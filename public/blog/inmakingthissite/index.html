In making this site...

2026-10-21

  • The Vision

After many scatterbrained attempts at creating a website with meaningful content between my many, many side projects, I am fairly certain this one will stick.

However, I can't seem to find anything to say despite the nagging sensation that 'I need somewhere to write'. Is it internalized fear of being forgotten in the sea of bodies, constructing nothing useful to impact others and leave a mark in the shifting sands of time? Or maybe it is simply ego, to feed some starving need for validation or give in to the ever-present cacophany of digital voices screaming to 'do more, now, forever'?

Whatever it is, thanks for finding it. Glad to have you here, kind stranger.

The Vision

Yes, it most likely is. I slapped it together in ~2 hours sitting in a library after lecture, and I will be tinkering with it for weeks until it's refined. I'm not a design student after all. But in the spirit of design:

10: Good design is as little design as possible.

Dieter Ram, Ten Principles for Good Design

Or, in cruder terms, Keep It Simple, Stupid. Words to live by.
